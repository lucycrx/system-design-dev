## Design Context

### Users
PMs, vibecoders, and founders learning system design — not engineers prepping for interviews. They arrive curious but potentially intimidated by technical content. The interface should signal "you belong here" without dumbing anything down.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, systematic, intentional. Dieter Rams meets a design monograph — sharp, grid-based, and precise. Premium but not exclusive. Smart but not intimidating. The typography does the talking.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme**: Light mode only. Warm off-white backgrounds (`#FAFAF8`), white surfaces, deep charcoal text (`#1A1A1A`).
- **Palette**: Bold institutional palette inspired by LaB K. Neutral black/white foundation with a warm red accent (`#C44536`) used sparingly for highlights and hover states — never for primary CTAs. Four core colors drawn from the reference: red (`#C44536`), steel blue (`#4A708E`), forest green (`#256B3B`), golden ochre (`#C49A1D`). Two derived colors for additional modules: wine (`#8C3B3B`), slate (`#4E5E6E`). Each has a dim-background variant at ~10% opacity for tinted containers.
- **Typography — two modes**:
  - **Display** (`.heading-editorial`): DM Sans, bold 700, uppercase, tight negative tracking (`-0.02em`), line-height 1.02. Used for all page titles, card titles, and section headings.
  - **Labels** (`.label-mono`): DM Mono, 11px, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (`0.15em`). Used for all metadata, badges, section labels, nav items, and small UI text. Monospace is a co-equal voice in the design, not a secondary accent.
  - **Body**: DM Sans, regular weight, normal case. Used for descriptions, paragraphs, and long-form content.
- **Geometry**: Sharp edges everywhere. No border-radius on cards, badges, buttons, callouts, or interactive elements. Rectangular, precise, systematic.
- **Borders & Surfaces**: Warm gray borders (`#E0DDD8`). Cards are flat bordered regions, not elevated containers — no shadows, no rounded corners. Depth is communicated through border weight and background tinting, not elevation.
- **CTAs & Buttons**: Primary actions use dark fill (`bg-text text-bg`) with mono uppercase tracking. Red accent is reserved for hover states and inline highlights. Ghost/text links for secondary actions.
- **Progress indicators**: Flat 2px bars with dark fill. No rounded ends.
- **Interactions**: Subtle and restrained. Border-color transitions on hover, no shadow effects. Transitions at 0.2s ease.
- **Imagery**: No emojis. Use text labels, subtle SVG icons, or typographic treatments instead.
- **Future**: Iceberg visualization for the hero section (designed in Figma, not code-generated). Tasteful mascot characters planned (Duolingo-inspired but refined and not childish).

### References
- LaB K identity (Landesburo fur Bildende Kunst NRW) — bold institutional color palette, systematic grid typography
- Sutera (Awwwards) — editorial typography, monospace annotations, connector-line diagrams, sharp geometry
- Vitsoe (vitsoe.com) — restraint, whitespace, permanence
- Bang & Olufsen (bang-olufsen.com) — premium minimalism, typography hierarchy
- Dieter Rams' product design — systematic grids, sharp edges, function over decoration

### Anti-references
- Childish education apps (bright colors, cartoon illustrations, rounded bubbly UI)
- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient heroes, feature grids, social proof walls)
- Dark dev-tool aesthetics (neon accents, terminal vibes, hacker culture)
- Soft/rounded UI trends (pill buttons, large border-radius cards, pastel gradients)

### Design Principles
1. **Subtract, don't add.** Every element must earn its place. When in doubt, remove.
2. **Warmth through restraint.** Approachability comes from generous space and clear language, not decorative elements.
3. **Typography carries the hierarchy.** Two modes — editorial display and mono labels — do all the structural work. Size, weight, case, and font-family create hierarchy, not color or decoration.
4. **One accent, used sparingly.** Red accent appears only for hover states, inline highlights, and small emphasis. Primary actions use dark fill, not accent color.
5. **No emojis.** Use text labels or minimal SVG icons. Keep the tone cerebral.
6. **Sharp geometry.** No border-radius anywhere. Rectangles, straight lines, flat edges. Precision over softness.
